About Fire Math Calc

Fire Math Calc is a free set of early-retirement calculators that show the formula behind every result.

Fire Math Calc exists to make the arithmetic of saving, investing and retiring early easy to check. Many calculators hand you one number and hide how they got it. Every tool here prints its formula, labels each assumption as yours, and shows the worked steps, so you can follow the math and change any input.

The site is written and maintained by Fire Math Calc. It is an independent educational resource. Nothing on it is investment, tax or legal advice, and it does not recommend stocks, funds or other products. Future returns, withdrawal rates and inflation are unknown. The calculators treat them as inputs you choose, not predictions. For decisions about your own money, consider speaking with a qualified professional.

Official figures come from primary sources: the Bureau of Labor Statistics for consumer prices and the IRS for contribution limits. Research is cited by author, publication and year, and only after the original has been read. The methodology page lists each formula, each data source with its date, and when the data is refreshed.

Corrections. If you find an error in a formula, a figure or a source, please report it through the contact page. Include the page address and what you believe is wrong. Reported errors are checked against the original source. Confirmed mistakes are fixed promptly. Calculator changes are re-tested against their worked examples before they go live.
