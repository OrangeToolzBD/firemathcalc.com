Privacy policy

Last updated September 20, 2026.

What you type into our tools is processed in your browser and is not sent to us. The tools do not store your entries. If you press “Copy link to this result”, your entries are placed in the copied link after the # sign so the person you share it with sees the same result; that part of a link is not sent to our server.

Analytics, advertising and partners

How we measure visits. This site does not currently run Google Analytics or any other visitor-tracking service. If that changes, this page will be updated first to name the service, what it collects and how to opt out.

What happens to your calculator entries. The numbers you enter into a calculator are processed in your browser and are not sent to us. They leave your browser only if you press “Copy link to this result.” That button builds a link with your inputs after the # sign, so the same calculation opens again when the link is used. Browsers do not send that part of a link to web servers, but anyone who receives the link can see the figures in it.

Advertising and affiliates. At launch, this site has no advertising partners and no affiliate partners. If that changes, this page will be updated before any are added, naming each partner, what it collects and how to opt out.
