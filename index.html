Where the numbers come from

Every calculator here shows its formula, and every figure it relies on comes from a named source. Inflation uses the Bureau of Labor Statistics CPI-U (all items, U.S. city average), with annual averages from 1913 to 2025 plus the 2026 months published so far, last downloaded September 16, 2026. The 401(k) and Roth IRA tools apply the IRS limits for 2026, announced November 13, 2025. The 4% withdrawal rate is explained with the original research: Bengen (Journal of Financial Planning, 1994) and Cooley, Hubbard and Walz (AAII Journal, 1998). Returns, withdrawal rates and inflation forecasts are always your own assumptions. CPI data is refreshed after each monthly BLS release, and contribution limits are checked each November. The site is maintained by Fire Math Calc. The methodology page lists every formula and source.
